EAA Applicability Checker

A free two-minute check of whether the European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882) likely applies to your business.

How this works. You answer six questions about your markets, your customers, and what you sell. The checker compares your answers against the scope lists in the European Accessibility Act and gives a plain-language result with next steps.

What this is not. This is general guidance based on the text of Directive (EU) 2019/882. It is not legal advice. The Act applies through national transposition laws, which differ between EU countries, and market-surveillance authorities make the final call.

Q4 — Headcount

Q5 — Annual turnover or balance sheet total

What is your annual turnover or balance sheet total?

Under Article 3(23), a microenterprise employs fewer than 10 people and has annual turnover not exceeding EUR 2 million OR an annual balance sheet total not exceeding EUR 2 million.

Q6 — Accessibility today

Where are you today on accessibility?

This affects your next steps only, never the verdict.
